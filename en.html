Chicken Please Privacy Policy

Effective: December 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026. The Korean version is the governing text.

UBTop ("we") protects the personal information of players of the mobile game Chicken Please ("the Game") under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws.

1. Information we process and how

We do not collect your name, email, phone number or address. While you play, the app and our partners automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID / iOS IDFA, when you allow it)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, country) and app version
  • Gameplay records (progress, play time, purchases and ad views), errors and performance data
  • If you use cloud save: an anonymous player ID and your game progress

2. Purposes

  • Running the Game, saving and restoring progress, fixing errors and improving performance
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Showing and measuring ads (personalized ads only with your consent)
  • Preventing abuse and verifying purchases

3. Consent and your right to refuse

On first launch the Game asks for consent to collect and use data for personalized ads and analytics. You can refuse and still use every feature; you will then see non-personalized ads and analytics is not sent to partners or is minimized. You can change your choice any time in Settings.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is deleted without delay when its purpose is met, when you withdraw consent or when you delete the app. You can delete your cloud save and account in Settings. Data sent to partners is kept under each partner's retention policy (e.g. Google Firebase Analytics, up to 14 months as configured).

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your data at any time, in the Game's Settings or at the contact below. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

7. Children

The Game is made for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 and delete it as soon as we learn of it.

8. Security

Encrypted transport (HTTPS), least-privilege access and certified partner services.

9. Paid items and withdrawal

Unused paid items can be withdrawn (refunded) within 7 days of purchase through Google Play or the App Store refund process. The Game has no chance-based (random) paid items.
